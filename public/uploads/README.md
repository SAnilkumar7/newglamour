## 🖼️ How to Add Your Photos Manually

1. Simply copy or drag your image files into the respective subfolder:
   - Example: Place a bridal look photo at `public/uploads/reviews/bride-1.jpg`
   - Example: Place a portfolio photo at `public/uploads/portfolio/bridal-look-1.jpg`
   - Example: Place the artist portrait at `public/uploads/artist/shwetha-subhash.jpg`

2. Supported image formats:
   - `.jpg` / `.jpeg`
   - `.png`
   - `.webp`

3. Recommended Dimensions for Perfect Fit:
   - **Review / Look Portraits**: `1080 x 1350 px` (4:5 vertical portrait aspect ratio)
   - **Portfolio Photos**: `1080 x 1350 px` (4:5) or `1200 x 1200 px` (1:1 square)
   - **Hero & Studio Banners**: `1920 x 1080 px` (16:9 widescreen) or `1600 x 1000 px` (16:10)

## ⚡ In-Browser Upload (Even Faster!)

You can also upload photos directly from your browser without manually touching files:
1. Open the website and go to **Studio Admin (`/admin`)** or the **Reviews (`/reviews`)** page.
2. Click **"Upload & Manage Photos"** or **"Add Review with Photo"**.
3. Select any image from your computer or phone — it will instantly preview and reflect across the website!

## 🎯 Smart Photo Framing Guarantee

All photos are automatically balanced by the website's built-in `SafeImage` smart framing engine:
- **No Over-Zoom**: Facial symmetry, maang tikka, forehead styling, and necklaces are preserved.
- **No Distortion**: Photos never stretch or compress.
- **Optical Balance**: Container dimensions scale gracefully across mobile, tablet, and desktop screens.
